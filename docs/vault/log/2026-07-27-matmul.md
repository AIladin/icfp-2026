## PADK fold

Before moving the large rooms, PADK has a free structural cut. Its normal token path turns south at
`d`; its once-per-row reset path continued six cells east and then paid the full return. The reset
suffix can turn north into the row used only during startup:

```text
@rMbv....
.v..<WbW<
.>rsmd1s^
.^...<...
```

On the reset path, `1 s` emits PAD, then the westbound `W b W` restores `A=1, B=K, BP=K` before
rejoining at `v`. The ordinary path is byte-for-byte the old `d -> <` return. This takes PADK from a
12x4 to a 9x4 interior and removes six walking ticks per inserted PAD. Implemented in
`py/matmul_gen6.py`; it also frees the east side for the conservative repack.

## 46x46 construction

The first 47x47 sketch still paid a column solely for the ring feed. The ring can keep v5's planar
embedding without it:

- ACC becomes 22x11 and ACCR/OUT move two rows north. The bottom crossing still forces row 45, so
  height 46 is the topology floor.
- The ring drum uses x36..44 on ordinary rows. On rows 39..42, where ACC/ACCR leave a dead eastern
  band, it extends west to x28. Those four bumps buy 32 cells, allowing the feed to descend at x45
  rather than x46. The resulting drum has 239 cells.
- Flow still enters the drum at its south-east end and exits at `(36,22)`, then runs north in the
  one-cell corridor beside MUL. MUL, LOADER and ACC keep their v5 coordinates.
- The ring pair retains a programmatic `>=273` capacity assertion. The gate drum loses 39 cells but
  retains a `>=90` assertion against the documented ~84-cell drain requirement.
- Raising ACC's south wall would tie the north `prod` pipe and `accb` at the prod receive. Moving
  the single-incoming accb pin from x18 to x17 restores a one-cell margin; both acc-ring receives
  retain margins of at least five.

The intended box is **46x46**. Unlike rejected v4, this does not translate the three binding-heavy
rooms; nevertheless it moves pipes and therefore still requires a server round trip.
