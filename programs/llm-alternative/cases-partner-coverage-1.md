# Partner coverage suite 1

`cases-partner-coverage-1.json` contains 12 programs handcrafted from scratch. All grids are within
16x16. Expected frames were generated by `py/llm_model.py`; parser/shape assertions also checked the
intended room, pipe and man counts. Additional generation-time assertions checked global wall stop,
independent halting, pipe length, blocking, nearest-pipe selection, negative receipt and simultaneous
receipt.

## Gaps

This suite targets successful runtime semantics. It intentionally does not cover structural load
errors, bad-op/no-pipe errors, display validation, maximum population, or server scoring limits.
Those require separate verdict-oriented suites rather than expected-frame conformance cases.
